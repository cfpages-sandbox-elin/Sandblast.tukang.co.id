## Position and boundary

The site should help owners, designers, facility teams, installers, and buyers choose, design, install, maintain, and procure frosted privacy film. It does not own true abrasive/acid-etched glass fabrication, safety-glass engineering, or unsupported optical/security claims.

## Evidence gates

- Glass type, film compatibility, thermal stress, breakage, fire/egress, safety/security performance, and warranty require glass identification, manufacturer approval, and tested assembly evidence.
- Privacy, VLT, haze, UV, heat, colour, day/night visibility, life, and cleanability require named product data and representative site samples.
- Artwork/logos require rights and owner approval. Prices, schedules, and warranties require dated written terms.
- Cracked/chipped glass requires isolation and competent assessment; no DIY safety-glass modification.
